Department of Vocational Studies in Healthcare

Comprehensive educational frameworks designed to prepare medical instructors, lab technicians, and healthcare administrators.

Department Overview

Vocation in Healthcare Management, commonly known as Healthcare Management, is a Diploma, Postgraduate, and Undergraduate degree programme that deals with handling administrative tasks and managing a healthcare institute.

Every healthcare organisation requires professionals who can look after office operations, accounting, marketing, PR, inventory management, HR management, customer satisfaction and many such services. To fulfil these job roles, students are trained through the Healthcare Management programme. After completing this course, a candidate will possess management skills and knowledge to carry out professional responsibilities with efficiency and effectiveness.

Key Features of B.Voc (Bachelor of Vocation)

The Bachelor of Vocation (B.Voc) is a three-year undergraduate degree program focused on practical, industry-ready skill development rather than purely theoretical education. Unlike traditional degrees, B.Voc ensures direct alignment with National Skill Qualification Framework (NSQF) occupational standards.

Curriculum Split (Theory vs. Practical)

The course structure generally includes 40% general education (theory) and 60% vocational training (practical / on-the-job training). This ratio ensures students spend the majority of their studies building real-world expertise and hands-on skills in partner healthcare facilities.

Multiple Entry and Exit Options

Aligned with national UGC guidelines, students can exit early at different milestone levels with valid qualifications:

  • 1 Year: Exit with a Diploma
  • 2 Years: Exit with an Advanced Diploma
  • 3 Years: Graduate with a full B.Voc Degree

Eligibility Criteria

Students who have completed 10+2 (Higher Secondary) in any stream, or who hold an ITI/polytechnic diploma, are fully eligible to apply for direct admission into our vocational streams.

Popular Specializations

Core industries aligned with B.Voc structures include Software Development, Retail Management, Healthcare & Paramedical Operations, Hospitality & Catering, Digital Marketing, and Green Energy trades.
