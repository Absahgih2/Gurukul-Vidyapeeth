Program Overview

The D.Voc in Cardiac Care Technology program at Gurukul Vidyapeeth University Namchi, Sikkim provides rigorous training, modern diagnostics practices, and academic knowledge co-designed with top industries and healthcare providers. It provides extensive lab hours, practical workshops, and direct guidance under our eminent faculty.
