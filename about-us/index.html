Overview & Genesis

Our Journey of Transformation

From a humble beginning to a recognized university — a story of vision, perseverance, and commitment to education.

2007 — The Beginning

Dev Sanskriti Mahavidyalaya

The institution began its journey under the aegis of the Ved Mata Gayatri Shikshan Samiti (founded in 2007) as Dev Sanskriti Mahavidyalaya, laying the foundation for value-based education in the heart of Chhattisgarh.

2011–2012 — Expansion

Dev Sanskriti University of Education & Technology

The institution expanded its academic scope and vision, evolving into the Dev Sanskriti University of Education & Technology, broadening its offerings to include engineering, management, and vocational programs.

Present Day — Recognition

Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya (DSVV), Chhattisgarh

Now a state-private university established under the Chhattisgarh Private Universities Act and recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC), DSVV stands as a beacon of holistic education that harmonizes modern academic rigor, technology, and professional skills with ethical, cultural, and spiritual development.

Location & Campus

Where Learning Meets Nature

A serene, eco-conscious campus nestled in the educational corridor of Durg, Chhattisgarh.

Prime Location

Khapri (Rural Area), Dhamdha Main Road, District Durg, Chhattisgarh — with administrative and campus links across the Sankra, Kumhari, and Raipur–Durg corridor.

Excellent Connectivity

Approx. 6 km from Durg Railway Station and just 2 km from the permanent IIT Bhilai Campus (Kutela Bhanta), ensuring seamless access for students and visitors.

Eco-Conscious Campus

A sprawling, plastic-free and tobacco-free campus featuring landscaped gardens, open green spaces, dedicated vehicle sheds, and secure perimeters — a truly sustainable learning environment.
