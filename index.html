Centralized Digital & Physical Library System

Gurukul Vidyapeeth University features a comprehensive centralized library system designed specifically for technical research and engineering literature:

  • Digital Access Tokens: Students receive cryptographically secure digital library tokens for remote access to IEEE, ACM Digital Library, and Springer engineering journals.
  • 25,000+ Physical Volumes: Core textbooks, reference materials, technician manuals, and trade reference works.
  • High-Speed Study Pods: 24/7 quiet study rooms equipped with high-speed fiber internet and power stations.
