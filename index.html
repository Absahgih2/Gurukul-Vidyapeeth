Government Recognized & Legally Shielded Higher Education

Established under the Sikkim Legislative Assembly Bill (March 2024), Gurukul Vidyapeeth University operates under the statutory framework of Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956.

UGC Act Section 2(f) Protection

Degrees, postgraduate certificates, and engineering diplomas awarded by the university carry legal validity across all Indian State & Central Public Sector examinations (UPSC, GATE, State PSCs) and global evaluations.

  • High-Speed Study Pods: 24/7 quiet study rooms equipped with high-speed fiber internet and power stations.
